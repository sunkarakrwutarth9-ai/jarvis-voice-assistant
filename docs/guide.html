J.A.R.V.I.S.

A voice-controlled AI assistant for Windows. Say “OK Jarvis” — it acts, builds, researches and talks back in English, Telugu or Hindi.

GET STARTED

Install in three steps

1

Download & extract

On GitHub click Code → Download ZIP. Right-click the ZIP → Extract All.

2

Double-click run.bat

First time only, it installs everything automatically (Python if missing, a private environment, AI models). Takes 5–15 minutes.

3

Paste a free key

Jarvis asks for a Google Gemini key. Get one free at aistudio.google.com/apikey. Then say “OK Jarvis”.

Next time just double-click run.bat — or Start Jarvis.vbs to start silently with no window. Look for the thin glowing line at the top of your screen: that's the Dynamic Island.

VOICE

Things you can say

“OK Jarvis, play Believer”Plays it on YouTube. “Pause”, “next”, “skip the ad”, “like it”.
“Open Chrome and open Gmail”Shows your Chrome accounts — pick one by voice or click.
“Write a Python program that…”Written live on the canvas. “Run it” shows the output.
“Build a snake game” · “make a website for my shop”Playable / clickable right inside the command center.
“Make a 3D globe of the Earth” · “a solar system”Interactive 3D you can drag to rotate and scroll to zoom.
“Make a piano” · “simulate gravity”Music apps, drum machines, simulations and animations.
“Remind me to call Mom at 6 pm”Reminders and alarms (“wake me up at 6:30 on weekdays”), spoken when due.
“Add milk to my shopping list”Shopping, to-do or any list. “What's on my list?”
“When I say good night, mute and lock the PC”Routines: one phrase runs several steps.
Play any music 🎵A little robot dances to the beat in the corner of the screen. Clicks go straight through it, and it hides during full-screen video. “Hide the robot” / “make the robot dance”.
“Tell me a joke” · “quiz me”Jokes, quizzes, conversions, meanings and translations.
“Make the button blue” · “make it shorter”Edits whatever is on the canvas, as a new version.
“Research electric cars in India”Reads the web and writes a report with sources.
“Summarise my resume PDF”Reads PDF, Word, text and code files.
“Open WhatsApp and search for Mom”Autopilot: looks at the screen and clicks / types step by step.
“Good morning Jarvis”Time, weather, battery, top headlines and your reminders.
“Remember my exam is on Monday”Long-term memory. “Forget my exam” removes it.
“Set volume to 30” · “lock the PC”Volume, brightness, windows, screenshots, timers, sleep.
“ఈరోజు వాతావరణం ఎలా ఉంది?”Speak Telugu or Hindi — Jarvis replies in your language.

Conversation mode: after “OK Jarvis” it keeps listening — just keep talking. Say “deactivate” to make it stop listening. Nothing Jarvis creates is saved unless you say “save it”.

COMMAND CENTER

The cinematic dashboard

Say “open command center” (or visit http://localhost:7777). A living 3D atom — glowing nucleus, racing electrons — reacts to your voice; around it are your system vitals, the conversation, and the canvas where creations appear with Preview · Code · Run · Save · Full screen. Themes: Cinematic, iOS (light/dark) and Iron Man — just ask.

PERSONAL

Your own voice and face

Record a 10–20 second selfie video of yourself talking, then run
.venv\Scripts\python.exe build_avatar.py "C:\path\to\video.mp4"
Click the round avatar next to the island (or say “talk in my voice”). Say “learn my voice” for an even better match. Everything stays on your PC.

HELP

Troubleshooting

ProblemFix
Doesn't hear “OK Jarvis”Raise the microphone level (Settings → System → Sound → Input, 80–90%). Or click the island / press Space in the command center.
“Application Control policy has blocked this file”Windows Smart App Control blocked a library. Jarvis works around it automatically; English speech may be ~1 s slower.
Slow or “rate limited”Free Gemini keys have daily limits; Jarvis switches models by itself. You can add an extra provider key in .env.
Anything elseRun run.bat console to watch the live log, or open jarvis.log.
